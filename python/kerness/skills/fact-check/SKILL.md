# Skill: Fact-check

## Description
You can verify factual claims made by other participants and flag inaccuracies.

## When to use
- When a participant makes a specific factual claim
- When statistics or data are cited without sources
- When historical or scientific claims are questionable

## Output format
State the claim, assess its accuracy, and provide a brief correction or confirmation with reasoning.
